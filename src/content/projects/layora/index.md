Most sleep apps hand you the same white noise track no matter how you're feeling. Layora asks first: how loud is your mind right now? Then it walks you down a different path depending on the answer.

Racing thoughts keeping you up? Dump them out of your head, breathe through a slow 4-7-8 pattern, and drift off to rain that gradually fades to silence. Too wired to sit still? Trace a few constellations with your finger until your hands — and your heart rate — settle. Just plain exhausted? One tap is all it takes: a softly pulsing light and a fading ambient wash while your phone dims itself out of the way. A Morning Vault keeps whatever you let out the night before, ready to read back over coffee.

Available now on Android, with iOS on the way. Built end-to-end with Claude Code.
